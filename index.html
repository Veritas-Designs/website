Investigate. Measure. Understand.

Veritas aims to help students bridge the gap from middle school to univeristy and professional STEM careers. We focus on real observations and give parents practical support for meaningful STEM learning.

Areas we're exploring

In person instruction

Conceptual understanding, problem solving, and scientific reasoning for high-school and AP Physics learners.

Hands-on labs

Investigations that invite students to make predictions, collect measurements, analyze data, and explain results.

Small-group learning

Exploring ways to support homeschool families and co-ops through collaborative STEM experiences.

Meet the people behind Veritas

We're a Maryland family building a new program to support homeschool high schoolers through AP-level STEM coursework, online lessons, in-person labs, and a community of students learning together.

Coach Ashley Keefe

Ashley is the founder of Veritas Designs and a homeschool mom of two. She brings a background in kinesiology and previously held certifications in USA Powerlifting and training.

Dr. Kevin Keefe

Kevin is a physicist and former state-certified high-school teacher and adjunct professor. He has taught AP Physics, high-school chemistry and biology, and undergraduate physics and astronomy.
