OUR APPROACH

Expert instruction. Family-directed learning.

Get expert help with advanced subjects without having to teach every course yourself. Remain involved in your student's learning path while we build understanding for college-level study and STEM pathways.

Through problem solving and scientific investigation, students explore why ideas work, connect mathematics with evidence, and develop the reasoning and independence needed for further study.

Meet the people behind Veritas

We are a Maryland family developing a STEM homeschool academy around rigorous instruction, parental involvement, and the needs families share with us.

Ashley Keefe, CEO

A full-time homeschool mom of two, Ashley brings a background in kinesiology and experience as a certified powerlifting coach and trainer. She is building Veritas alongside Kevin, with a focus on keeping parents actively involved as their students pursue advanced math and science.

Kevin Keefe, Ph.D.

Kevin is a physicist, former state-certified high-school teacher, and former adjunct professor. His experience includes AP Physics, high-school chemistry and biology, undergraduate physics and astronomy, and graduate-level physics and electrical engineering.

His teaching range spans pre-algebra through differential equations and linear algebra, middle- and high-school science, and advanced physics. The initial course selection will depend on demand and delivery capacity.
